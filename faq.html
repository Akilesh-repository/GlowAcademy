Frequently Asked Questions

To apply, you must be at least 17 years old and hold a high school diploma or a GED. Prospective students are required to submit an application form, attend a personal interview with our admissions team, and pass a basic entrance evaluation to ensure readiness for our rigorous curriculum.
Yes, Glow Academy is a fully accredited institution recognized by the Department of Education. We offer federal financial aid, including Pell Grants and Direct Student Loans, to those who qualify. Additionally, we provide in-house zero-interest payment plans and various institutional scholarships.
Our Master Cosmetology program requires 1,500 clock hours. Full-time students typically complete the program in about 10 to 12 months attending classes Monday through Friday. Part-time and evening students usually take between 16 and 18 months to finish.
Absolutely. We have a dedicated Career Placement Director who assists with resume building, portfolio creation, and interview prep.
