Who We Are

More Than Just A Beauty School.

Glow Academy is an elite institution built on the belief that beauty is an empowering blend of science, art, and personal expression. We don't just prepare you for state boards; we prepare you to be an innovator, a trendsetter, and a leader in top-tier salons and spas across the globe.

Our distinguished educators bring decades of real-world expertise directly into the classroom, providing personalized mentorship that unlocks every student's unique creative potential.

25+Years Elevating Careers
Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide an exceptional, comprehensive education that empowers students to achieve their absolute highest potential in the beauty and wellness industry through innovative teaching, rigorous practice, and an inclusive, supportive community.

Our Vision

To serve as the globally recognized gold standard for excellence in cosmetology and esthetics education, producing well-rounded graduates who lead the industry with innovation, unbreakable integrity, and exceptional technical skill.

Our Legacy

A History of Excellence

1999

The Foundation

Founded in New York with a single classroom and a bold vision to transform local beauty education standards.

2005

Curriculum Expansion

Successfully added comprehensive Esthetics and Advanced Nail Technology programs to our core offerings.

2015

State-of-the-Art Campus

Moved to our current 20,000 sq ft facility, featuring high-end modern clinic floors and digital labs.

2022

National Recognition

Awarded 'Academy of the Year' by the National Beauty Educators Association for graduate success rates.
