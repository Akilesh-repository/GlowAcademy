Funding Your Education

Financial Aid Options

Don't let tuition stand in the way of your passion. We are a fully accredited institution, meaning our students have access to a variety of federal and state financial aid programs.

Federal Grants (Pell Grants)

Need-based grants that do not have to be repaid.

Direct Student Loans

Low-interest loans available to eligible students.

Glow Academy Scholarships

Merit and need-based institutional scholarships up to $2,000.

Flexible In-House Payment Plans

For students paying out of pocket, we offer interest-free monthly payment plans to help manage the cost of your education while you study.

Standard Plan

20% Down Payment required before classes start. The remaining balance is divided evenly into monthly payments over the length of your program.

  • 0% Interest Rate
  • No Credit Check Required

Extended Plan

10% Down Payment required. Balance divided into smaller monthly payments extending up to 6 months after graduation.

  • Lower Monthly Payments
  • Soft Credit Check Required (5% Admin Fee)

Need Help Navigating Financial Aid?

Our dedicated financial aid advisors are here to help you fill out your FAFSA and explore all funding options available to you.
